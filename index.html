📖 How the pieces move

Pawn: One square forward, or two from its starting square. Captures diagonally forward. Promotes on the last rank.

Knight: Jumps in an L shape: two squares plus one sideways.

Bishop: Moves diagonally. Rook: Moves horizontally and vertically. Queen: Both.

King: One square in any direction without moving into check.

Special rules include castling, en passant, queen promotion, checkmate and stalemate.

🧠 Before every move
  1. Is my king in check or a piece under threat?
  2. Do I have any checks, captures or threats?
  3. If I play this, what is my opponent's best reply?
  4. After that reply, what will I do next?
🏆 Your long-term goal

The goal is checkmate, not capturing the queen. Opening moves are patterns, not universal rules.

We cannot measure your genetic chess ceiling. Your ambition has no artificial limit here, and the academy tracks progress through actual performance.
